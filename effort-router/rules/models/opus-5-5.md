- The default level is medium (earlier Opus models defaulted to high).
- Levels think more here than the same names did on earlier models: medium beats Claude Opus 5 at high on coding and knowledge-work evaluations, and low comes close to that on several coding evaluations at much lower cost.
- At a given level it thinks more per turn than Claude Opus 5, most of all at xhigh and max. Keep xhigh and max for work where the extra rigour clearly pays.
