Our ledger and the bank's settlement report disagree by a few pence for some accounts in September. Find out why and fix it.
Make chargeMonthlyFees safe to run on two servers at the same time. Today the only guard is the ledger's duplicate key, and we're moving to a database that doesn't have one.
Add retries for failed payouts: back off exponentially, give up after 5 attempts, and never pay twice if the bank's response to a request was lost.
Payouts can now be in a different currency from the account. Convert at the FX rate at settlement, debit the account in its own currency, and charge the fee on the converted amount.
Do a security review of the CLI and the exporters: can anyone see or change another account's money?
Change every amount in the codebase from a number to a bigint without changing any output, so we can hold amounts above 2^53.
Refunds can be partial and can be split across the original payment and its fee. Work out how refunds should hit the ledger and implement it.
